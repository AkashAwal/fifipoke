ABOUT FIFIPOKE 

Hellu, I’m Fifi a tattoo artist based in New Delhi, working with both handpoke and machine tattoos. Whether you’re after a delicate, minimalist design or a bold, custom piece, my focus is always on precision, hygiene, and creating something meaningful for you.

​

As a curious mixed-media artist, I stumbled into the world of tattooing and was instantly drawn to its raw authenticity and the intimate connection it creates with the human canvas. From painting on canvas to etching designs onto skin, I’ve always searched for ways to turn imagination into something tangible and lasting.

​

What inspires me most are your stories, passions, and identities each tattoo becomes a collaboration that expresses who you are. Whether it’s a subtle reminder close to your heart or a bold statement piece, I’m here to translate your vision into art that stays with you forever.


MY APPROACH
Precision
I prioritize precision and attention to detail. Each tattoo reflects personalized customization, ensuring clients' visions are realized. My aim is to exceed expectations, delivering unique artistry that speaks to individual style.

Hygiene
I ensure top-notch hygiene and safety protocols. My workspace is sanitized, and I use disposable materials for client well-being. With strict adherence to safety measures, I ensure a secure environment for every tattoo session.

To book your tattoo, pick a design from the flash sheet or request a custom one. A non-refundable booking fee of ₹700 is required to lock in your slot and secure your design — this amount will be adjusted in your final tattoo cost.

​

If you need to reschedule, please inform me at least 1 day in advance. Rescheduling more than once will require starting the booking process again, including a new booking fee.

For any questions, check out the FAQ page or feel free to DM me directly before booking.
